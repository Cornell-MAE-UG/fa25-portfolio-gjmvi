# MAE 3870 Project

## Overview

The course MAE3780 is an opportunity for an applied learning mechatronics experience. The capstone of this course was to partake in a robot competition, in which, in teams of three, we designed an Arduino-based robot that would collect blocks on a color-mapped surface. The project consisted of a series of milestones, and the final objective was to compete against a second robot on the same surface to collect a majority of the blocks. It was an enriching and holistic experience, and it helped to deepen the electronic foundation we learned during the semester. The complexity of the robot was primarily responsible for this, as it truly required complete circuit layups, sensors, and feedback. The complexity of the circuit also required that the code be fully developed and that masking be correct and complete. It was an all-around challenge and concreted the beginnings of our mechatronics engineering journey.
